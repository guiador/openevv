# Unicode CLDR emoji names

This application includes Unicode CLDR 48.2.3 annotations and derived annotations for Spanish (`es`) and Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), filtered against Unicode Emoji 17.0 sequences. Copyright notices and the Unicode license are included in `app/src/main/assets/unicode/LICENSE.txt` and `unicode/cldr-48.2.3/LICENSE.txt`.

Original data and source hashes: `unicode/cldr-48.2.3/manifest.json`.
Reproducible import: `tools/update-unicode-emojis.py`.

Sources: https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json/releases/tag/48.2.3 and https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/emoji/emoji-test.txt.
